About Me

Hi, I'm Marina

I hold a Bachelor of Arts in Mass Media and Communication, with an additional focus in Psychology, which gives me a unique perspective on my work. My understanding of psychological principles enhances my ability to craft compelling narratives that resonate deeply with diverse audiences. This expertise has been instrumental in my years of experience in top marketing positions, including my role as a Marketing Events Director across different industries.

In addition to my work as a content creator, I am an accomplished graphic designer and social media marketing expert. My background in psychology informs my approach to audience engagement, allowing me to create content that truly connects with people on an emotional level.

I film New York like I never got used to it. Every Reel is shot on iPhone, edited with intention, and built to make people feel something about this city.
